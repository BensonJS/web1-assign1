About me:

I am Cree person, I moved here from my community up north about three years ago to study film and media production. I was very passionate about film and it always was my dream to work in this field, mostly to tell people about my culture and community. My family is very important to me, my grandparents rasied me to be the person I am today and I LOVE THEM SO MUCH!

I am currently working on a few projects with some friends and I am excited to share them with you when they are ready to be published. You can also see on my YouTube channel a documentary I made when I was going to Film and Media Productions, It was about my grandfather's struggles afther coming back residential school. And how him and my grandmother supported each other through those difficult times. It was a very meaningful project for me and I am happy to share it with you.
